1 Grammar — Adverbs and adverbial phrases

  
Mountain adventure: complete the gaps with suitable adverbials, then choose your path. Numbers link to the next scene.

  
Tip: Gaps ask for time, manner, degree, comment, or place adverbials (e.g. suddenly, extremely, unfortunately, in the cave).


  

    
Adventure path 0/12


    

      1
      
You are hiking through the mountains . There's  going to be a storm. Do you …?

      

        → carry on walking → 8
        → take cover in a cave → 20
      

    


    

      2
      
You move the rock .  Behind it there is a polished door. What do you do?

      

        → Put the rock back → 13
        → Go inside → 19
      

    


    

      3
      
The figure approaches you . Huge, white and hairy –  it is the famous Yeti! What do you do next?

      

        → Take a selfie of you and the Yeti → 6

      4
      
 Your phone won't work. You're getting  desperate when you see something or someone . Do you …?

 Yetis don't like publicity. The Yeti smashes the camera  and runs away .

        → follow them → 12

The hikers stop you .  they demand your money and valuables. They run away and leave you on the mountain.

      

        → Go to 10
      

    


    

      10
      
Darkness comes  and you are  lost and  cold. Mountain Rescue will find you  if you are lucky. Your journey ends here.

    


    

      11
      
 This is stealing! Are you  sure?

      

        → Yes, in fact I'll take more gold → 18
        → No, I'll just take a photo of it → 14
      

    


    

      12
      
You walk together. The route seems  strange and you walk for ages. Suddenly, one of the hikers says, , ‘we're lost’. You decide to …

      

        → run away from them → 10

      13
      
 You hear voices coming towards the cave. Someone is shouting something .

      

        → Run away before the people see you → 10
        → Wait and see → 7
      

    


    

      14
      
You leave the cave  and  find your way down the mountain. You tell the authorities about the secret room and become famous for discovering it. Good choice!

    


    

      15
      
The storm has become an icy blizzard! It's getting  dangerous. You …

      

        → telephone for help → 4
        → walk even more quickly → 10
      

    


    

      16
      
You sleep  and wake up . A huge white and hairy creature – the Yeti – is looking at you . Do you …?

      

        → apologise and get out of the cave → 8

      17
      
It is  a human but he or she is  huge! You have  seen someone so big. You …

      

        → shout, ‘Help!’ → 3
        → run away → 10
      

    


    

      18
      
You leave the cave  and struggle through the storm but you don't get very far with all that weight on your back.  You have to abandon your rucksack.

      

        → Go to 10
      

    


    

      19
      
 You find yourself in a palatial room! There's a vast four-poster bed . What do you do next?

      

        → Lie down and sleep → 16

      20
      
You clamber  into a nearby cave. There is a large rock  which seems to have been moved . Do you …?

      

        → roll away the rock → 2
        → sit and wait for the storm to pass → 13
      

    


    

      21
      
The Yeti takes you on a secret path . You come out of the mountains . Well done, you're safe and you can say you met a Yeti!

3 Speaking — Find someone who…

  
Walk around and find classmates who match each description. Write their name and a detail. Use perfect forms naturally.


  

    
Past — Find someone who, by the time they started school, …

    

      
#PromptNameDetails

      
        
1had moved flat/house more than once

        
2had started to speak two languages

        
3had met their best friend

        
4had learned how to read and write at home

        
5had thought about their future profession

      
    

  


  

    
Present — Find someone who, this year, …

    

      
#PromptNameDetails

      
        
6has won a prize or competition

        
7has worked or studied too hard

        
8has travelled to any really interesting places

        
9has not had much good luck

        
10has done something for the first time

      
    

  


  

    
Future — Find someone who, by the end of the year, …

    

      
#PromptNameDetails

      
        
11will have saved up enough money for something particular

        
12will have celebrated an important anniversary

        
13will have achieved something really worthwhile

        
14will have taken lessons in something new

        
15will have prepared or trained hard for something
